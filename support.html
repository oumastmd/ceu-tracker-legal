CEU Tracker: CE Logbook — Support

CEU Tracker is a simple personal logbook for the continuing education (CE / CEU) hours your licenses require. Enter your license's renewal date and hours once, log trainings, attach certificates, and see what's left before renewal.

Common questions

Is CEU Tracker an official tool?

No. It is a personal tracking tool and is not affiliated with any licensing board or official organization. It counts what you enter, using the rules you enter, and never reports to anyone. Always check your board's own requirements.

How do I turn on iCloud Drive backups?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud › Drive, and turn on “Sync this iPhone”. Then in CEU Tracker open Settings › Backup and restore and tap “Back up to iCloud Drive”. Backups appear in the Files app under iCloud Drive › CEU Tracker.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Make a backup on the old iPhone (Settings › Backup and restore). On the new iPhone, install CEU Tracker, open Settings › Backup and restore, choose the backup and confirm. You can also export all your data as one file and restore from that file.

The scanner says camera access is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, find CEU Tracker, and turn on Camera. You can also import a PDF or photo instead, or choose a picture from Photos.

How do I delete my data?

In CEU Tracker, open Settings and tap “Delete all my data”. Your logbook and certificates are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud Drive if you chose to back up). See our Privacy Policy for details.
