CEU Tracker: CE Logbook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary. CEU Tracker does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The licenses, trainings, hours and certificate files (scans, photos, PDFs) you add. They are stored only inside the app on your device.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only while you scan a certificate. Photos you choose are copied into the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Backups

If you choose “Back up to iCloud Drive”, a backup file is saved in your own iCloud Drive account and is governed by Apple's terms. If you export a file, you decide where it goes. We never receive your backups.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no third‑party analytics or tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Not an official tool

CEU Tracker is a personal tracking tool and is not affiliated with any licensing board or official organization. It does not give professional or legal advice; check your board's rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
